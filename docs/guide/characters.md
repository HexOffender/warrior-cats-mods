# Characters

## What it changes in the game

Cats of your own come into the forest when you play a sandbox cat. Each wears the name and title you
gave it over its head, has the look you chose, stands beside your cat or at a spot you picked, and says
your lines when you walk up to it and use the game's *Talk* prompt. The Questlines page uses them to
hand out quests and to talk to.

How it gets there: your cats are saved in your mod, Apply puts them in the game, and Sandbox mode, a mod
of its own, brings them into the forest with a sandbox cat. Nothing in your save is changed.

## A five-minute first try

1. Open **Characters** and press **Add a cat**. The new cat already has a look of its own.
2. Type a name in **Name**, say Mossfoot, and a title in **Title** if you like (Elder).
3. Under **Colours**, tick **Coat** and choose a colour.
4. Type a line or two in **What they say**, one line each.
5. Press **Save to mod**. The strip under the Editing mod bar says whether Sandbox mode is ready; if it's
   in your mods but off, saving turns it on and says so.
6. Press **Play** (**Apply and play** when there's something to apply).
7. On the game's main menu choose *Sandbox Mode*, then make a new cat or choose one of your sandbox cats.
8. Mossfoot stands beside your cat. Walk up and use the *Talk* prompt (E, or A on a controller);
   Interact or Enter shows the next line.

### Getting Sandbox mode

The strip at the top of the page says where Sandbox mode stands:

- **Sandbox mode 0.5 is on**: nothing to do.
- **Sandbox mode is off**: press **Turn it on**.
- **Sandbox mode is left out since the game's update**: it was made for an older version of the game, so
  Apply leaves it out. On the **Mods** page, its card has **Use it anyway…**; then Apply.
- **Sandbox mode is in, but mods that run code aren't allowed**: it's a script, so allow mods that run
  code on **Settings and repair**, then Apply.
- **You'll need Sandbox mode**: download `sandbox-0.5.zip` from its releases page (the strip links it)
  and drop it on the **Mods** page.
- **Your fixed looks need Sandbox mode 0.5 or newer**: your cats go in, but an older Sandbox mode makes
  their look up each time. Get the newer one the same way.

Your mod goes in the game without Sandbox mode all the same (its items, palettes and names with it); only
the cats wait for it.

## What you can and can't do

You can:

- give a cat a name, and a title shown smaller above it;
- keep the **Same look every time** (its shape, pattern and colours chosen once and kept; **Fine-tune**
  changes each part, and the colour number each colour comes from) or give it a **New look each time**, as
  the game does with its own cats;
- choose its coat, pattern, markings, nose, paws, ears and whiskers colours on top of that, its eye colour
  (or **Game's choice**), and its size, from apprentice size to bigger than a leader;
- have it stand **Beside your cat** (the first time a sandbox cat comes into the forest, and there after
  that) or **At one spot** for every sandbox cat (**Use where my cat is standing** reads the spot from the
  game while it runs);
- give it lines to say when it has no quest for you.

You can't, yet:

- make a cat walk, follow you or patrol: it stands where it's put;
- make it smaller than an apprentice, or give it scars or accessories: nobody has tried those on the
  game's cats for company yet;
- see its shape and pattern on the page: the drawing shows its colours only;
- give it answers to choose from when you talk;
- meet it in a story (*Prophecy Mode*) game: only sandbox cats see your cats.

## Did it reach the game?

- After Apply, the log's summary names your mod. If Sandbox mode is missing, off, or kept out by mods
  that run code, the log says the cats went in but nothing shows them yet, and what to do.
- In the game, your cats are beside a sandbox cat a second or two after it comes into the forest. The
  mod menu (Insert) has a **Questlines** page from Sandbox mode, which lists your cats and can bring one
  beside you again.
- A line under the Editing mod bar that starts "Saved, but it can't go in the game yet" says what to fix.
- A cat with the same look every time that looks different each time means Sandbox mode is older than
  0.5: the strip says so.

## Common mistakes

- **Playing a story cat.** Your cats only come into *Sandbox Mode* games.
- **Pressing G.** Older versions talked with G. Now it's the game's own *Talk* prompt: E, or A on a
  controller.
- **At one spot, with no spot.** Until all three numbers are filled in, the cat stands beside your cat;
  the **Not ready yet** note at the top of the cat says so. Press **Use where my cat is standing** with
  the game running, or type the numbers.
- **Removing a cat a quest uses.** The message names the quest: choose another cat there, on the
  Questlines page, before Apply.

## Undo

- Before saving: switch to another mod in the Editing mod bar and back, and the page reloads what's
  saved (it asks first).
- After saving: change the cat and save again, or **Remove** it and save.
- After Apply: switch the mod off on the Mods page (or remove the cats) and Apply again; the cats are gone
  the next time a sandbox cat comes into the forest.
- Where a cat stood beside each sandbox cat is remembered by Sandbox mode. The mod menu's Questlines page
  brings one beside you again.

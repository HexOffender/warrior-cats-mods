# The Mods page

## What it changes in the game

The Mods page decides which mods are in the game. A mod switched on here goes in the next time you press
**Play** or **Apply to game** (top right); a mod switched off comes out. Nothing in the game changes until
then.

From top to bottom, the page has:

- **Get set up**: the things to do once, each with a tick. When they're all done it folds to one line, and
  **Show the steps** opens it again.
- **Mods that go with Clan Mod Studio**: four mods of their own that bring what you make into the game.
  [Their page](companion-mods.md) says what each one does.
- **Add a mod**: where a mod's .zip goes, from a friend or a download.
- **Your mods**: every mod in your mods folder, with a switch each.

How it reaches the game: Play copies what your mods make into the game's folder, under names of their own,
so the game's own files are never changed.

## A five-minute first try

1. Open Clan Mod Studio. The Mods page opens first, with **Get set up** at the top.
2. Watch it find the game and make a safety copy of your save. If it asks **Where's the game?**, paste the
   game's folder in (in Steam: right-click the game, Manage, Browse local files).
3. Press **Install it** for the script loader.
4. Press **Allow** on **Let mods run their own scripts**, read what it says, and press **Allow mods that
   run code**.
5. Press **Add all four**.
6. Press **Play**. In the forest, press Insert: the mod menu opens, with the pages about your cat.
7. Back in the studio, drop a mod's .zip on **Drop a mod's .zip here**, press **Add to my mods**, then
   **Play** again.

## What you can and can't do

You can:

- Add a mod from its .zip, or pick a few out of a .zip that holds many. The preview shows what each one
  changes, whether it runs code, and what else it needs, before anything is added.
- Choose whether each one goes in switched on. A .zip that holds several says how each one ships (ten
  themed palettes off, say), and you can change any of them.
- Add a folder: put it in your mods folder (**mods folder**, under Add a mod), then **look again**.
- Switch mods on and off. A mod that needs another switches that one on too, and switching off a mod that
  others need asks first.
- Find a mod by its name, its author or the words in its description, in **Find a mod**.
- Give a mod a priority, open its folder, save it as a .zip or remove it, under its **More**.

You can't:

- Change what's in the game while the game is running: the game keeps its files open. Close it, change
  what you like, press **Play**.
- Put in a mod that needs a mod you don't have. It's added switched off, and its card says what it needs.
- Put in a mod made for a newer Clan Mod Studio. Its card says "Needs Clan Mod Studio 0.5.0 or newer":
  get the newer Clan Mod Studio (unzip a new download over the old folder; your mods stay where they are).
- Put in a mod that runs code without allowing mods that run code. Its card says so.

## Did it reach the game?

- Under Get set up, "Not in the game yet" lists what's waiting (a mod on, a mod off, a mod changed, a mod
  left out and why, or a reason to apply again, such as mods that run code being allowed since the last
  time), with **Apply to game**. When nothing is waiting, it says "The game has your mods as they are
  here".
- The chip at the top says "Game up to date" once the game has everything.
- **Last apply** (top) opens what the last Apply or Play did: which mods went in, by name, or what kept
  one out.
- A mod's card says in red why that mod isn't in the game: it needs a mod that's off or missing, it was
  made for an older version of the game, or it needs a newer Clan Mod Studio.
- In the game, the mod menu's Installed mods page lists what went in.

## Common mistakes

- **"I added it but nothing changed."** Press **Play** (or **Apply to game**) after adding: the banner
  under Get set up says what's still waiting.
- **"It says it went in switched off."** The toast says why, for example "Added Moonlit cats, switched off:
  it needs Sandbox mode". Add the mod it needs (one of the four has its own **Add** on their card), then
  switch the mod on.
- **"I have two copies of the same mod."** An older Clan Mod Studio named a downloaded mod after its file
  name, version and all. Add the newer .zip: it replaces the old copy now, and the preview says "Replaces
  the Cool Mod 1.2 you have".
- **"Your mods go into another copy of the game."** Steam starts a different copy than the one your mods
  go into. Press **Use Steam's copy**.
- **"Close the game, then apply."** The game was still running. Close it and press **Apply to game**.
- **"The game updated", and a mod is left out.** A mod made for an older version of the game that is only
  data is checked on the new one when the page opens ("Checking your mods on the new version…"); the ones
  that build go in with the next **Play**. One that doesn't build says why: **Turn it off**, and wait for
  its maker. One that runs code waits for its maker too, or **Use it anyway…** after its warning.

## Undo

- Before adding: **Cancel** on the preview, and nothing is added.
- After adding: switch the mod off, or **Remove** it (under its **More**). A mod you remove or replace is
  kept: **Put it back**, on the Settings and repair page, brings it back (switched off).
- A step of Get set up you said **Not now** to: **Show the steps** opens the card again. The scripts step
  can be changed at any time under Options, on the Settings and repair page.
- After Play: switch the mod off and press **Play** again; it comes out of the game. What a mod wrote into
  your save stays in your save: put back a save backup to undo that ([Settings and repair](settings.md)).

# Names

The Names page gives your cats the names you choose, changes the names the game hands out to new
cats, and renames words the game shows. Everything you change is saved in your mod as `names.json`.

## What it changes in the game

- **The cats in your save.** Type a new name for any cat, yours included. The save gets the new name
  when you press Apply to game (after a backup of it is made).
- **The parts of clan names.** A clan cat's name is two parts: a prefix and a suffix (Otter + paw).
  The suffix changes with rank: kits are -kit, apprentices -paw, leaders -star. Rename a part and
  every name with it changes (Otterpaw, Otterheart and Otterstar become Mothpaw, Mothheart and
  Mothstar). Add a part and the game starts handing it out to new cats. Remove one and the game stops
  handing it out (cats that have it keep it).
- **Rogue and loner names.** Cats outside the clans have one whole name, from their own list.
- **The ban list.** Names the game never gives a new cat, mostly the books' characters.
- **Any other name.** The cameo cats (Brightheart, Rock...) and any text that's exactly a name.
- **Places, quests and items.** The areas' names (the one that pops up as you walk into it), the
  quests' titles and the items' names, each picked from a list.

Renames change the English text. The other languages keep their own translations. Names you add show
in every language, in English.

## A five-minute first try

1. Open **Names**. Under **Cats in your save**, find your cat (it's first) and type **Sparkle** in its
   New name box, then press Tab. The line under the box says "Sparkle at every rank": a name that
   isn't a prefix + suffix stays the same as your cat grows up.
2. Tick **grows with rank**. Now the line says your cat's name for its rank, Sparklepaw for an
   apprentice: Sparkle has become your cat's own prefix, and the end changes as it ranks up.
3. Press **Save to mod**, then **Apply to game**, then **Play**. Your cat's new name shows on screen
   while you play, and on the Select Your Cat screen.

## What you can and can't do

You can:
- give any cat in your save a new name: a prefix + suffix the game has (Mothheart), a name of your
  own (Sparkle), or a prefix of your own that grows with rank;
- give a rogue or loner a rogue name, or any name of your own;
- rename, add and remove prefixes, suffixes and rogue names, and ban or allow names;
- rename a cameo cat, or any name the game shows;
- rename an area, a quest's title (each rank's, for a quest whose title changes as your cat ranks up)
  or an item, from **Places, quests and items** at the bottom of the page. Open it, find the one you
  want, and type its new name.

You can't (yet):
- make the game hand out a whole name of your own to new clan cats (Luna for a new kit). The game
  builds clan cats' names from the two lists, so a new name for new cats has to be a new prefix or
  suffix;
- rename a cat while the game is open: close it, Apply, and play again;
- change names in other languages;
- add a line for a cat to say, or change a quest itself (only its title).

**A name of your own is text your mod adds to the game.** The game shows it only while that mod is
on. Keep the mod on, and keep it on when you share the mod with friends.

## Did it reach the game?

- The column **After Apply** says what each cat will be called once you Apply. A changed cat never
  shows a blank there: if the game would have no words for the name, it says **no text** and the cat
  keeps the name it has.
- **What this does**, near the top, counts the names that change and lists the cats being renamed, with
  anything worth checking.
- After Apply to game, the list shows your cats' new names straight away.
- In the game, your cat's name shows on screen while you play and on the Select Your Cat screen.
- A renamed area, quest or item shows in the mod menu's pages. The game's own screens (the area's name
  as you walk in, the quest log, your inventory) haven't all been checked yet: if one still shows the
  old name, the mod menu's pages still show yours.

## Common mistakes

- **A cat shows as Cat 1** (or as `<MISSING STRING TABLE ENTRY>`). The save has the cat's new name, but
  the game has no words for it, because the mod that named it is off. The page says so on the cat's
  row ("name from a mod that's off") and in a box above the list. Turn that mod back on and Apply, or
  press **Give it a game name** on the cat's row (it offers the name the cat had), Save to mod and
  Apply.
- **A typo in a name.** Mothhart isn't Moth + heart, so it becomes a name of its own that never
  changes. The line under the box says which: "Moth + heart: grows with rank", or "Mothhart at every
  rank".
- **Renaming a word that's also something else.** Rock is a cameo cat and a crafting material, and Fox
  is a rogue and an animal. Any other name renames every text that's exactly that word: the line under
  it says what else changes ("also changes the material Rock").
- **Two parts with the same text.** Renaming Otter to Wren gives two prefixes called Wren, and the
  page warns about it.
- **Taking out an earlier rename.** If a cat would lose its name, the page asks first.

## Undo

- **Before you Save to mod:** switch to another mod in the Editing mod bar and choose Switch without
  saving, or reload the page.
- **A rename you saved:** clear the cat's box (or press Reset on a part's row), Save to mod and Apply.
  A cat renamed in your save keeps its new name until you rename it again: type its old name in the
  box, Save to mod and Apply.
- **Everything at once:** your save is backed up before every Apply that renames cats. On **Settings
  and repair**, Save backups puts one back. **Take all mods out of the game** takes your mods out (it
  says first which cats would lose their names).

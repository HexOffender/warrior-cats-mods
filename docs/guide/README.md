# The guide

Help for Clan Mod Studio, a page at a time. The Help button at the top of the studio opens these in a
panel.

- [How Clan Mod Studio works](studio.md): your mods folder, the Editing mod bar and My mods, Save to
  mod, Apply to game and Play, the log, what Undo means here, and where your files are. Start here.
- [Items](items.md): herbs, materials and accessories of your own, with their icons, what they do and the
  medicine cat's recipe.
- [Animations](animations.md): the game's own animations as emotes on a key, or in another's place, and
  your own from Blender.

The rest of what wcmods can do is written up in the repository's docs, which open on GitHub:
the [README](../../README.md) (getting set up, the mod menu in game, remapping keys), the
[file formats](../formats.md) each page writes, and [docs/README.md](../README.md), which lists every
page and who it's for.

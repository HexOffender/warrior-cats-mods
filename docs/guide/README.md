# The guide

Help for Clan Mod Studio, a page at a time. The Help button at the top of the studio opens these in a
panel.

- [How Clan Mod Studio works](studio.md): your mods folder, the Editing mod bar and My mods, Save to
  mod, Apply to game and Play, the log, what Undo means here, and where your files are. Start here.
- [Palettes](palettes.md): recolour the eyes, coats, patterns and markings every cat picks from, and add
  new pattern and marking colours.
- [Your cat](cat.md): a look of its own for your cat, or any cat in your save: colours, the game's
  colours it wears, its shape and its scars.
- [Controls](controls.md): which key, mouse button or controller button does what, a second key for an
  action, keys in menus, and what the page says before you apply.
- [Names](names.md): renaming the cats in your save, the parts of clan names, rogue names, the ban list
  and any name the game shows; what to do when a cat shows as Cat 1.
- [Items](items.md): herbs, materials and accessories of your own, with their icons, what they do and the
  medicine cat's recipe.
- [Animations](animations.md): the game's own animations as emotes on a key, or in another's place, and
  your own from Blender.
- [Characters](characters.md): cats of your own for Sandbox mode, their looks, where they stand and what
  they say, and getting Sandbox mode.
- [Questlines](questlines.md): quests with those cats, step by step, with rewards, and starting them over.
- [Game data](data.md): the numbers the game plays by, from running speed to scars; Quick changes, the
  search, and every table.
- [Save](save.md): your own save, your cat's level, the fresh-kill pile and what it carries; each change
  made once, and put back.

The rest of what wcmods can do is written up in the repository's docs, which open on GitHub:
the [README](../../README.md) (getting set up, the mod menu in game, remapping keys), the
[file formats](../formats.md) each page writes, and [docs/README.md](../README.md), which lists every
page and who it's for.

# Questlines

## What it changes in the game

Your cats from the Characters page hand out quests to a sandbox cat. A cat with a quest for you wears
the game's quest mark; talking to it starts the quest; the step you're on shows in the game's objective
panel on the left, with a marker on the map where to go; and at the end the reward is dropped at your
cat's paws. A quest can wait for others to be done first, so quests tell a story.

How it gets there: your quests are saved in your mod, Apply puts them in the game, and Sandbox mode runs
them for each sandbox cat and remembers how far it has got. Nothing in your save is changed.

## A five-minute first try

1. Make a cat on **Characters** first (say Mossfoot) and save it, in the same mod.
2. Open **Questlines**, press **Add a questline** and type "Moss for Mossfoot" in **Title**. **Given by**
   is Mossfoot already, and the first step is a **Talk** with Mossfoot.
3. Type two lines in **What they say**, one line each.
4. Under **Add a step**, press **Pick up**, then choose Moss and 2.
5. Press **Talk** again and type a thank-you line; under **Reward**, add an item.
6. Press **Save to mod**, then **Play** (**Apply and play** when there's something to apply).
7. On the game's main menu choose *Sandbox Mode*, then a new cat.
8. Walk up to Mossfoot and use the *Talk* prompt; Enter shows the next line. The objective panel says
   "Find 2 Moss"; pick them up, talk to Mossfoot again, and the reward drops at your paws.

Sandbox mode has to be in your mods and on: the strip at the top of the page says what to do, as on
[Characters](characters.md#getting-sandbox-mode).

## What you can and can't do

You can:

- make steps that are done in order: **Talk** to one of your cats, **Pick up** some of an item, or **Go
  somewhere** (a spot in the forest, or the nearest medicine cat);
- make a quest wait for others under **Starts after**;
- give a reward of items, herbs and materials of your own included (from the Items page);
- write your own words for a step's **Objective**, or leave it empty for the words shown, which use the
  names your cats and items have when you Apply;
- say what the quest is about in **About it**, shown under its title on the mod menu's Questlines page
  (with Sandbox mode 0.5 or newer).

You can't, yet:

- put a quest in the game's own quest list: it shows in the objective panel and on the mod menu's
  Questlines page instead;
- make a step that takes the items away when you hand them in: they stay in your inventory;
- make a step to hunt, fight or climb, or give experience as a reward;
- give the player answers to choose from when a cat talks;
- send the player anywhere but a spot or the medicine cat by name.

## Did it reach the game?

- In the game, a cat with a quest for you wears the game's quest mark, and the objective panel shows the
  step you're on. The mod menu (Insert) has a **Questlines** page listing every quest with how far you've
  got ("Not started yet", the step, or "Done").
- After Apply, the log says if a quest names an item the game hasn't got and no mod adds.
- A line under the Editing mod bar that starts "Saved, but it can't go in the game yet" says what keeps
  the quests out, such as quests that wait for each other in a circle; the **Not ready yet** note at the
  top of the quest says it as you work, and marks the step.

## Common mistakes

- **Quests that wait for each other.** If A starts after B and B after A, neither ever starts. The page
  won't let you tick the box that closes a circle, and says so if one is in the file.
- **A talk step with nothing to say.** Each talk needs at least one line.
- **A cat from another mod.** Quests use the cats saved in the same mod: choose that mod in the Editing
  mod bar on both pages.
- **It's done already.** A sandbox cat that finished a quest doesn't get it again. Start it over (below).

## Undo

- Before saving: switch to another mod in the Editing mod bar and back (the page asks first).
- After saving: change the quest and save again, or **Remove** it and save.
- After Apply: switch the mod off on the Mods page and Apply again.
- How far each sandbox cat has got is Sandbox mode's, kept beside the game's saves. The mod menu's
  Questlines page starts one quest over; **Start every questline over**, under **Starting over** on this
  page, starts them all over for every sandbox cat while the game is closed. With Sandbox mode 0.5 or
  newer, a quest whose steps you change starts over by itself.

# Mods that go with Clan Mod Studio

## What it changes in the game

Four mods go with Clan Mod Studio. Each is a mod of its own, made by the people who make the studio,
and each brings something into the game that the studio's pages make or need:

- [Menu pages](README.md#menu-pages): pages in the in-game [mod menu](README.md#mod-menu) (Insert) about
  your cat, the world, quests, fights and cheats.
- [Sandbox mode](README.md#sandbox-mode): *Sandbox Mode* on the game's main menu, a cat in any Clan with
  no story. The cats and quests you make on the Characters and Questlines pages come into the game there,
  and only there.
- Creator palettes: the new colours your palettes add, and their coat presets, as choices in the cat
  maker's colour pickers, in a row under the game's. It comes with ten themed palettes, which go in
  switched off, and Coat presets, which goes in on. A palette of yours with the same folder name as one
  of those is left as it is, and the message after adding says so.
- Emote wheel: hold its key for a wheel of your emotes and the game's own poses.

How they get there: each one runs inside the game, so each needs the
[script loader](README.md#script-loader) and [mods that run code](README.md#code-mods) allowed. Clan
Mod Studio downloads them from their own pages on GitHub, so adding one needs the internet. They're a
card on the Mods page, **Mods that go with Clan Mod Studio**, not a page of their own; its
**What they do** opens this page.

## A five-minute first try

You need the script loader first: **Install it**, in **Get set up** on the Mods page.

1. Open the Mods page and find the card **Mods that go with Clan Mod Studio**.
2. Press **Add** on the Sandbox mode row. Its chip changes to "In, 0.4", or to **Needs scripts allowed**.
3. If it says **Needs scripts allowed**: open Settings and repair, tick **Allow mods that run code**
   under Options, read the warning, and press **Allow mods that run code**.
4. Close the game if it's open, then press **Play** (top right; it reads **Apply and play** while changes
   wait).
5. On the game's main menu, choose *Sandbox Mode*.

## What you can and can't do

You can:

- Add all four at once with **Add all four** in **Get set up**, or one at a time with **Add** on its row.
  While Sandbox mode is missing, the Characters and Questlines pages have **Add Sandbox mode** in the
  strip at their top too, and Animations will have **Add the Emote wheel** once that one is out.
- See where each one stands from its chip: **Not added**, "In, 0.4" (in your mods, on, and the newest
  the studio knows of), **Off**, **Needs scripts allowed**, "Newer one out: 0.6",
  **Needs a newer Clan Mod Studio** or **No release yet**. When all four are in, on and up to date, the
  card folds to one line, and **Show them** opens it.
- Ask GitHub with **Check for newer versions**, then update one with **Get the latest** (on its row, or
  under its card's **More**). An update keeps whether it's on and your own settings (Menu pages'
  cheats, the Emote wheel's key), and the older copy is kept with your removed mods.
- Add one from a .zip you downloaded yourself: drop it on **Drop a mod's .zip here**, like any mod. Your
  settings are kept that way too.
- Switch any of them off on its card under **Your mods**, like any mod.
- Keep Menu pages, Creator palettes and the Emote wheel as they are while you play a
  [mod pack](packs.md) on its own: they're your own tools, so **Play only this pack** leaves them alone.

You can't:

- Add one that has no release yet. Its row says **No release yet**, with a link to its releases: the
  owner of that mod has to publish one first. Nothing is wrong on your side.
- Choose an older version: **Add** and **Get the latest** always take the newest release. For another
  one, download its .zip from its releases page and drop it on **Drop a mod's .zip here**.
- Put one in the game that needs a newer Clan Mod Studio than yours. Its row says
  **Needs a newer Clan Mod Studio**: get the newer studio first (unzip a new download over the old
  folder; your mods stay where they are).
- Have them in the game while mods that run code aren't allowed: they all run inside the game.

### Which versions are out

- Menu pages 1.0 works with this Clan Mod Studio.
- Sandbox mode 0.6 is the newest. It's the one a cat with a route (**Where they walk**) needs (with an
  older one that cat stands still). A cat set to **Same look every time** needs 0.5 or newer (with 0.4 that
  cat gets a new look each time), and so does starting a quest over when you change its steps. If you
  have an older one, **Check for newer versions** shows "Newer one out: 0.6": press **Get the latest**.
- Creator palettes 1.2.0 and the Emote wheel 1.0 are out. Creator palettes' coat presets need this
  Clan Mod Studio (0.4.0 or newer). The Emote wheel opens on G. Neither has been tried in the game in
  this version yet.

## Did it reach the game?

- On the card, "In, 0.6" in green means it's in your mods and on. It reaches the game at your next
  **Play** or **Apply to game**; until then, "Not in the game yet", near the top of the Mods page, lists
  it ("Sandbox mode on").
- Menu pages: Insert opens the mod menu, with Your cat, World, Story, Fight and Cheats in its side bar.
- Sandbox mode: *Sandbox Mode* is on the game's main menu.
- Creator palettes: the cat maker's colour pickers have a row of your new colours under the game's.
- Emote wheel: holding its key shows the wheel.
- The mod menu's Installed mods page lists each one that went in.
- Not tried in the game yet: **Add all four** on a fresh install, then allowing mods that run code and
  pressing **Play**. If something doesn't show, the mistakes below are the first things to check.

## Common mistakes

- The chip says "Needs scripts allowed". Allow mods that run code (**Options**, on Settings and repair),
  then press **Play**.
- The chip says "Off". Press **Switch on** on its row, then **Play**.
- A message says "GitHub is turning requests away for now". GitHub answers only so many questions an
  hour. Wait an hour, or download the .zip from its releases page and drop it on
  **Drop a mod's .zip here**.
- A message says "No internet connection". Clan Mod Studio couldn't reach GitHub, so nothing was added,
  and the step in **Get set up** stays open. Press **Add all four** (or **Add**, or
  **Check for newer versions**) again once you're online.
- The chip says "No release yet". Nothing to fix: that mod's owner hasn't published it yet.
- The Characters page says your fixed looks need Sandbox mode 0.5. Press **Check for newer versions**
  on the Mods page, then **Get the latest** on Sandbox mode's row. Until then those cats get a new look
  each time, and only their looks wait for it: everything else comes into the game with 0.4. A route
  (**Where they walk**) says Sandbox mode 0.6 the same way: until then those cats stand still.

## Undo

- Before **Play**: switch it off on its card, or **Remove** it (under its **More**; press it twice).
- After **Play**: switch it off and press **Play** again: it comes out of the game.
- After **Get the latest**: the older copy is kept. **Remove** the new one, then press **Put it back**
  beside the old copy, under Removed and replaced mods on the Settings and repair page. It comes back
  switched off: switch it on.
- What you did in the game with them is part of your game, as anything you play is: a sandbox cat stays
  among your saved games. A save backup from before puts your save back
  ([Settings and repair](settings.md)).
- How far your questlines have got is kept by Sandbox mode beside your saves:
  **Start every questline over**, on the Questlines page, starts them all again.

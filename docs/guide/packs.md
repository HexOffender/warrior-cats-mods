# Mod packs

## What it changes in the game

A mod pack is a set of mods that go together as one experience: a story with its cats, quests and items,
the colours and names that suit it, new controls. In the game, a pack is just its mods: switching a pack on
switches its mods on, and **Play only this pack** plays them with every other mod of yours off.

How it gets there: a pack is a mod with nothing of its own in it but a list of its mods. Its .zip carries
every one of them, so whoever adds it on their Mods page gets them all.

## A five-minute first try

1. Make two things on the Make pages, say a palette and a few names. They save into My mods.
2. Open **Mod packs**.
3. Under **Make a mod pack**, give it a **Name**, and tick its mods under **Mods in it**.
4. Press **Make the pack**.
5. On the pack's card, press **Play only this pack**, then **Play** (top right).
6. When you're done, press **Back to my mods**.

## What you can and can't do

You can:

- Make a pack from any of your mods; the mods they need (Sandbox mode for characters and questlines, say)
  go in with them.
- Play a pack on its own (**Play only this pack**), or with your own mods (**Turn on with my mods**).
- Share it with **Save as .zip**: the .zip carries every mod in it. Whoever gets it drops it on their Mods
  page, where the preview shows the pack and its mods. Your My mods goes in under the pack's name, so
  theirs stays theirs; a script or coat preset that names "my-mods" inside it still does, so put those in
  a mod of their own before you share.
- Change it (**Change it**) or remove it (**Remove the pack**); removing a pack keeps its mods, and
  removing the pack you're playing brings your own mods back.
- See on its card what would keep one of its mods out of the game: a mod that runs code while that isn't
  allowed, one made for an older version of the game, one that needs a newer Clan Mod Studio.

You can't:

- Put a pack inside another pack: choose its mods instead.
- Share a pack while one of its mods is missing from your mods folder: its card says which.
- Turn your own tools off by playing a pack: Menu pages, Creator palettes and the Emote wheel (and any mod
  made as a tool) stay as they were while a pack plays on its own, so a pack needn't carry them.

## Did it reach the game?

- The pack's card says **Playing** while it plays on its own, and **On** while it's on with your mods.
- Its mods show as chips on its card: green when they're on.
- After **Play**, Last apply (top) names the mods that went in.

## Common mistakes

- **"Play only this pack is greyed out."** One of its mods isn't in your mods folder; the card says which.
  Add the pack's .zip again, or the mod on its own, or **Change it**: a missing mod is dropped from the pack
  when you save it.
- **"My friend's pack has a mod with the same name as mine."** The preview says "You have a different mod
  with this name". It's ticked when the pack's copy is newer; untick it to keep yours.
- **"My own mods are gone."** They're only switched off while a pack plays on its own: **Back to my mods**
  puts them back as they were.
- **"The story's cats don't turn up for my friend."** Characters and questlines need Sandbox mode, and it
  runs code: your friend needs it in their mods and mods that run code allowed.

## Undo

- Before making it: change the ticks, or leave the page.
- After making it: **Change it**, or **Remove the pack** (its mods stay).
- After **Play only this pack**: **Back to my mods** switches your own mods back on and the pack's off; then
  **Play**.

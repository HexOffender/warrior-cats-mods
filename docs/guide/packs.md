# Mod packs

## What it changes in the game

A mod pack is a set of mods that go together as one experience: a story with its cats, quests and items,
the colours and names that suit it, new controls. In the game, a pack is just its mods. Switching a pack on
switches its mods on; **Play only this pack** switches them on and every other mod of yours off, except
your own tools.

How it gets there: a pack is a mod with nothing of its own in it but a list of its mods. Its .zip carries
every one of them, so whoever adds it on their Mods page gets them all. (For people who write mod files,
the file is described in [Mod packs](../formats.md#mod-packs).)

## A five-minute first try

1. Make something on a Make page (a colour on Palettes, say) and press **Save to mod**. It goes into
   **My mods**.
2. Open **Mod packs** (in the side bar, under Your mods).
3. Under **Make a mod pack**, give it a **Name**. Under **Mods in it**, the mods you have on are ticked
   already: leave My mods ticked.
4. Press **Make the pack**.
5. On the pack's card, press **Play only this pack**.
6. Close the game if it's open, then press **Play** (top right; it reads **Apply and play** while changes
   wait).
7. In the forest, press Insert: the [mod menu](README.md#mod-menu)'s Installed mods page lists the mods
   that went in.
8. When you're done, close the game, press **Back to my mods** (at the top of the Mod packs page), then
   **Play** or **Apply to game**.

## What you can and can't do

You can:

- Make a pack from any of your mods. The mods they need, and the mods they work with that you have
  ([Sandbox mode](README.md#sandbox-mode) for characters and questlines, say), go in with them.
- Play a pack on its own (**Play only this pack**), or with your own mods (**Turn on with my mods**).
- Keep your own tools while a pack plays on its own: [Menu pages](README.md#menu-pages), and
  [Creator palettes and the Emote wheel](companion-mods.md), stay as they were, so a pack needn't carry
  them.
- Share it with **Save as .zip**: the .zip carries every mod in it, and your browser saves it (usually in
  Downloads). Whoever gets it drops it on their Mods page, where the preview shows the pack and its mods.
  Your My mods goes in named after the pack ("The Moonlit Path: its own content"), so theirs stays theirs.
- Change it (**Change it**, then **Save the pack**) or remove it (**Remove the pack**, pressed twice).
  Removing a pack keeps its mods, and removing the pack you're playing brings your own mods back.
- See on its card what would keep one of its mods out of the game: a mod that runs code while that isn't
  allowed, one made for an older [game build](README.md#game-build), one that needs a newer Clan Mod
  Studio.

You can't:

- Put a pack inside another pack: packs aren't in the **Mods in it** list. Choose their mods instead.
- Play or share a pack while one of its mods is missing from your mods folder: **Play only this pack** and
  **Save as .zip** are greyed out, and the card says which mod is missing.
- Keep your other mods on while a pack plays on its own, palettes included: only your tools, and mods the
  pack's mods work with that were on, stay on. **Turn on with my mods** is for playing it with yours.
- Share a coat preset or a script saved in My mods safely in a pack: after the pack's .zip renames My
  mods, they can still point at it by its old name. Make those in a mod of their own (**New mod…** in the
  Editing mod bar) before you share.

## Did it reach the game?

- The pack's card says "Playing" while it plays on its own, and "On" while it's on with your mods. That's
  the studio's side: the game has it after **Play** or **Apply to game**.
- Its mods show as chips on its card: green when they're on, red when one is missing. "(needed)" marks a
  mod that came along with the ones you chose.
- After **Play**, **Last apply** (top) opens the [build log](README.md#build-log), which names the mods
  that went in.
- In the game, the mod menu's Installed mods page lists them.

## Common mistakes

- **Play only this pack** is greyed out. Either the pack is playing already, or one of its mods isn't in
  your mods folder (the card says which). Add the pack's .zip again, or the mod on its own, or press
  **Change it**: a missing mod is dropped from the pack when you save it.
- Your friend's pack has a mod with the same name as one of yours. The preview says "You have a different
  mod with this name". It's ticked when the pack's copy is newer; untick it to keep yours (a copy you
  replace is kept in backups).
- Your own mods are gone. They're only switched off while a pack plays on its own: **Back to my mods** puts
  them back as they were, then press **Play**.
- The story's cats don't turn up for your friend. Characters and questlines need Sandbox mode (the pack
  carries it when you have it), and it runs code, so your friend has to allow
  [mods that run code](README.md#code-mods).

## Undo

- Before making it: nothing is saved until you press **Make the pack**.
- After making it: **Change it**, or **Remove the pack** (its mods stay).
- After **Turn on with my mods**: switch the pack off on the Mods page. Its mods stay on until you switch
  them off too.
- After **Play only this pack**: **Back to my mods** puts back the mods you had on before, and only those;
  then press **Play**.

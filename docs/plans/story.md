# Stories: the Characters and Questlines pages, with Sandbox mode

This plan covers the two story pages in Clan Mod Studio (`studio/sections/npcs.js`, `studio/sections/quests.js` and what they share in `studio/lib/story.js`), the format and build code behind them (`wcmods/story.py`, `wcmods/sections/story.py`, the routes in `wcmods/studio.py`), the parts of the in-game runtime they lean on (`wcmods/lua/game.lua`), and the Sandbox mode mod that runs them (its own repository, `D:/projects/wcmods-sandbox`, `sandbox/scripts/story.lua`). It was written from the 2026-10-28 audit maps for the story, separate-mods and lua-runtime areas, the skeptics' verdicts on every high and medium claim, the code on `main` at 3af2898 (which has PR #71, the Fixed look), and the three branches already under way as they are committed: `claude/studio-tests` 73840ad, `claude/studio-look` 4a597a8, `claude/studio-shell` c97edf4 (which has merged the look and is merging the tests).

Three pieces of work are under way in other branches and are referred to, not planned again. The test harness (`claude/studio-tests`): `tests/unit/conftest.py` with the `studio`, `fake_game`, `save` fixtures, `make_mod` and `call`, and an autouse fixture named `sandbox` that stubs the machine (it has nothing to do with Sandbox mode); HTTP handler tests; the Chromium driver `tests/studio/lib/browser.mjs` and a DOM shim `tests/studio/lib/dom.mjs`; `node:test` units under `tests/studio/` (CI runs `tests/studio/**/*.test.mjs`); it has already fixed `slug` for accents (`"Éclair Brûlée"` gives `eclair-brulee`) and its `tests/unit/test_studio_api.py` asserts today's `"requires": ["sandbox"]` on a story save. The look (`claude/studio-look`): Leaf and Bark, per-page `studio/sections/<page>.css`, `wc.pageHead({eyebrow, title, lede, help})`. The shell (`claude/studio-shell`): save-time checks through `Studio.check_file`, which runs the owning section's `collect()` on the saved mod, so the PUT answer becomes `{saved, requires, problems}` (a list, replacing `story_problem`'s single `problem`) shown under the Editing-mod bar; the dirty guard with Save/Discard/Stay on a mod switch and `beforeunload`; the plain-words Apply summary (`plainSummary` in `studio/app.js`, which reads the manifest's mods and warnings, not section notes); errors reworded for creators; help panels from `docs/guide/<page>.md` opened with `wc.openHelp(page)`; its `tests/unit/test_studio_shell.py` also asserts `"requires": ["sandbox"]`.

Three sibling plans written from the same audit touch this area, and each piece has one owner. `docs/plans/mods-setup.md` (Getting set up, the Mods page and the companion mods) owns the add-on registry (`GET /addons`, one-click Add and Get the latest for Sandbox mode), the `"wcmods"` minimum in mod.json, the `tool` flag that Play only this pack keeps on, and the version chips on the Mods page cards. Its step 3 adds an Add button and a version line to the Characters and Questlines banner; that banner is rewritten here as `sandboxStrip` (step 2), so its step 3 puts only the Add and Get the latest button into the strip, and the version wording is this plan's. Its step 8 releases Sandbox mode 0.5: that release is this plan's step 1, which also adds the `"wcmods": "0.3.0"` key that step 8 asks for. `docs/plans/console-lua.md` (scripts, the Console page and the in-game runtime) owns the runtime fixes for bugs 14 to 18 below in its step 1 (errors with the mod's name, deduplicated; the `runTasks` snapshot; `Game.onKey`; other cats' level; the dialogue claim, `hide()` and `talkId`), the `BridgeError` wording in its step 2, and in its step 5 a script mod's UE4SS.log lines on its Mods card, which is where Sandbox mode's own log lines reach a creator. `docs/plans/docs-guide.md` owns the creator guide pages `docs/guide/characters.md` and `docs/guide/questlines.md` (its step 7) and the guide's tests; this plan hands it the facts (step 9) and owns the reference docs (formats.md's npcs.json and quests.json sections, the README's "Stories without code"). The guide must not say that one saved cat makes the whole mod need Sandbox mode, which step 2 ends.

## What it is for

A creator who can't code makes cats of their own and gives them something to say and something to ask for, then plays it: the cats stand in the forest, talk when walked up to, and hand out quests that lead the player somewhere and reward them. They should be able to make the first cat and the first quest in five minutes, see them in the game after one Apply, and never be stopped by a message they can't act on.

## Where it stands

What works, and how it was verified. `wcmods/story.py` reads `npcs.json` and `quests.json` with a readable message for every mistake, including a loop of "Starts after" (`_no_loops`); `tests/unit/test_story.py` (25 tests, passing on 2026-10-28) covers the happy path, nineteen mistakes with their messages, Random versus Fixed looks, the `NPC_LOOK` and `NPC_SLOTS` tables in `game.lua` matching `catcolours`, the `WCModsStory` script round-tripping its JSON through `luacheck`, and a mod pack carrying a story mod. `tests/game/test_npcs.py` drives `Game.npcs.spawn` in the game: colours and the eye row painted, a destroyed cat respawned, a fixed look kept across respawns. `docs/verified.md` records the sandbox's studio cat wearing the game's Talk prompt with the real Interact key. The Sandbox mode README's "Tried in game" list (build 26184573) covers the script example's quest (`examples/sandbox-welcome`: talk, pickups, hand-in, reward, progress per cat), not a questline made on these pages; `verified.md` lists a questline's reward drop (with a mod item's own model on the ground) as not watched. Both pages save, reload, share as a .zip, and say what is wrong with a quest in a "Not ready yet" banner. Dialogue with more than one line already works: each line of the box is one screen and Interact or Enter shows the next.

What is half-finished. The Questlines page still describes the drawn `!` and the drawn quest panel, which are the fallback for a wcmods older than `Game.npcs.onInteract`; with this wcmods the cats wear the game's own marks and the game's objective panel. ## What a creator should be able to do

The Characters page is one cat at a time, chosen from the list on the left, in four cards with plain labels. "Who they are" has Name and Title. "How they look" opens with two choices, "Same look every time" (the default for a new cat: rolled once, kept) and "New look each time", a drawing of the face in the cat's colours, the colour ticks, the eye swatches with a "Game's choice" swatch, and the size slider labelled by the game's roles (apprentice, elder, warrior, leader, "bigger than a leader") instead of a bare percentage; the nine look numbers and the colour-slot rows sit under a "Fine-tune" fold with one honest sentence, that the shape and pattern show only in the game. "Where they stand" is "Beside your cat" or "At one spot", and a spot not filled in yet is said in a "Not ready yet" note at the top of the cat, not hidden; a typed spot needs no facing, since the cat turns to face your cat when it is first put there. "What they say" is the lines box, and "When they're about" is Always or a from-to pair of hours (step 8). Above all of it one strip says where things stand: "Sandbox mode 0.5 is on", "Sandbox mode is off" with a Turn it on button, "Sandbox mode is in, but mods that run code aren't allowed" with where to allow them, "You'll need Sandbox mode" with the exact file and the steps (the companion-mods plan turns that into one Add button), or "Your fixed looks need Sandbox mode 0.5; you have 0.4"; and under it one muted line with the whole route: "To see them: Apply to game, Play, Sandbox Mode on the main menu, then a new cat or one of your sandbox cats. Walk up and use the game's Talk prompt", with the help panel's link. The id and the raw JSON panel move under "For mod makers".

The Questlines page keeps its list and editor and reorders the cards: "The quest" (Title, About it, Given by), "Starts after", "Steps" (Talk, Pick up, Go somewhere, and Reach a level from step 8), "Reward" (items, and from step 8 experience, a heal, fresh-kill), with the "Not ready yet" note at the top of the open quest and the offending step marked. The same strip and route line sit at the top, and the route line adds that quests show in the left-hand objective panel and in the mod menu's Questlines page, not in the game's own quest list. A "Starting over" card says how progress is kept (per sandbox cat, in `sandbox-story.json` beside the saves), that a reworked quest starts over by itself, and has a "Start every questline over" button for when the game is closed.

The five-minute first try: open Characters, Add a cat, type Mossfoot, tick a coat colour, Save to mod (the strip says Sandbox mode is on, or the save turns it on and says so). Open Questlines, Add a questline, type "Moss for Mossfoot", the giver is Mossfoot already, the first Talk step is there with Mossfoot chosen, type two lines, add Pick up, choose Moss and 2, add Talk with one line, add Moss to the reward, Save. Apply to game, Play, Sandbox Mode, a new cat. Mossfoot stands beside you with the game's quest mark; Interact starts the talk, Enter goes on, the objective panel says "Find 2 Moss", the moss is picked up, the hand-in talk runs and the reward drops at your paws. Nothing in that path mentions a slot, an id, JSON, `--force` or `wcmods install`.

## Formats and compatibility

A mod made before keeps working; nothing needs converting by hand.

- `mod.json`: a mod an older studio saved has `"requires": ["sandbox"]`; it keeps the hard need (and today's refusal when Sandbox mode is missing) until it is saved again on Characters or Questlines, which moves it to `uses`, except for a mod with scripts of its own. `uses` is a key every wcmods already reads; an older wcmods doesn't turn a used mod on with it, and never blocks on it.
- `npcs.json`: `"at": null` is accepted by `story.py` today and not passed to the game; an older page shows it as "Beside your cat". `when` is new and optional (step 8).
- `quests.json`: a step without `objective` was always valid; the build now fills it in. An older wcmods leaves it to the sandbox's own words ("Find moss", without the count). `gives` and the `level` step are new and optional (step 8); an older studio refuses them with "unknown key(s)".
- `WCMods.story` keeps `"format": 1`; the new fields are additions an older sandbox ignores, and a sandbox from 0.5 on refuses a higher format in words.
- `sandbox-story.json`: per slot `made` and per quest `shape` are added; an entry without them takes today's values and keeps its progress, and Sandbox mode 0.4 reading a 0.5 file ignores both. The studio's start-over keeps the file's shape.
- `Game.quests.report`: the sixth field is ignored by readers that know five.
